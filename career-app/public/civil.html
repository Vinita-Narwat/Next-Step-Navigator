CIVIL ENGINEER

Civil Engineers design and maintain structures such as public and private buildings, water systems, airports and roads. Structural Engineers analyze, design, build and maintain the structures and materials that counteract or reinforce loads. Some of the tasks a Civil Engineer, is expected to perform include planning, organizing and supervising construction and repair of bridges, factories, towers, etc.; calculating and preparing estimated cost and detailed drawings; arranging for men, material, machinery and appliances, etc.

    Career Opportunities


  • Public Sector: PWD, NHAI, Indian Railways, municipal corporations, and government infrastructure projects.
  • Private Sector: Construction firms, real estate developers, consultancies, and multinational engineering companies.
  • Emerging Fields: Smart cities, green buildings, disaster-resilient infrastructure, and sustainable urban planning

Future Scope


  • Demand for civil engineers is expected to rise, especially in urban development and climate-resilient infrastructure
  • India’s infrastructure sector is projected to grow rapidly due to government investments in highways, metros, and housing.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
Diploma in Civil Engineering Completion of 10th grade from a recognized board. Suitable for early entry into technical roles.
B.E./B.Tech in Civil Engineering 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM). Entry through national/state-level entrance exams.
M.E./M.Tech in Civil Engineering Bachelor’s degree in Civil or related engineering field. Specializations available in structural, environmental, and geotechnical engineering.
Ph.D. in Civil Engineering Master’s degree in Civil Engineering or allied disciplines. Focus on research and academic careers.

Where will you work?

Places of Work: Local authorities, Government departments, Railways, Water/electricity/gas companies across India. You can also work as Civil engineering contractors/consultants across India.

Work Environment: Your job role will require you to travel locally, and handle a team of engineers. You will work for 8/9 hours per day and for 5/6 days per week.
