DIETICIAN

A dietician is a qualified health professional who specializes in human nutrition and the regulation of diet. They assess, diagnose, and treat dietary and nutritional problems at both individual and public health levels. Dieticians work in hospitals, clinics, wellness centers, sports organizations, and private practice.A Dietician is a registered professional, who studies the science of nutrition and uses this knowledge to advise people on their food and nutrition choices. Dieticians help their patients by correcting their eating habits with diet charts, meal-plans, etc. Dieticians create awareness around healthy consumption.

    Career Pathways


  • Clinical Dietician:Work in hospitals to plan diets for patients with diabetes, heart disease, or kidney issues.
  • Sports Nutritionist:Design performance-enhancing diets for athletes and fitness enthusiasts
  • Public Health Dietician: Work with NGOs or government bodies on nutrition awareness and community health.
  • Food Service Manager: Oversee meal planning and nutrition in schools, hotels, or corporate cafeterias.

Key Responsibilities


  • Deep understanding of human nutrition and metabolism
  • Communication and counseling skills
  • Empathy and motivational interviewing
  • Knowledge of food safety and dietary guidelines
  • Ability to create customized meal plans

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
B.Sc. in Nutrition/Dietetics 10+2 with PCB or Home Science. Covers human physiology, food science, and diet planning.
M.Sc. in Clinical Nutrition Specialization in Finance or Investment Analysis. Preferred for senior roles.Specialization in therapeutic diets, disease management, and advanced nutrition.
Internship Mandatory clinical training in hospitals or wellness centers.
