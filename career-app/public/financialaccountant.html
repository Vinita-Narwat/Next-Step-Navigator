FINANCIAL ACCOUNTANT

A Financial Accountant is responsible for running the accounting and financial activities of an organisation. They carry out the day to day financial work for a firm, prepare financial statements and conduct audits, They also advise on accounting matters and ensure proper maintenance of account books and record of business. A financial accountant is responsible for recording, summarizing, and reporting a company’s financial transactions to ensure accuracy, compliance, and strategic insight.

    Career Pathways


  • Corporate Accounting:Maintain financial records, prepare reports, and support audits
  • Public Accounting Firms: Handle client accounts, tax filings, and financial consulting.
  • Government & Regulatory Bodies: Work in financial compliance, budgeting, and public audits.
  • Financial Services: Join banks, insurance firms, or investment companies in accounting roles.

Key Responsibilities


  • Financial Reporting & Analysis
  • Knowledge of Accounting Standards (GAAP, IFRS)
  • Taxation & Compliance
  • Attention to Detail & Accuracy
  • Software Proficiency (e.g., Tally, SAP, QuickBooks)

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
Bachelor’s Degree In Accounting, Finance, or Business Administration. Covers financial reporting, taxation, and auditing.
Professional Certification Required for senior roles and statutory compliance. Adds credibility and global mobility.
Experience 1–3 years in accounting, auditing, or financial reporting roles.
