CORPORATE LEGAL ADVISOR

A Corporate Legal Advisor is an in-house legal professional who advises companies on legal matters related to contracts, compliance, intellectual property, mergers, employment law, and regulatory issues. Unlike external lawyers, they work directly within the organization to protect its legal interests.A Corporate Legal Advisor or a Compliance Officer ensures that all corporate processes and procedures comply with the law. They are responsible for ensuring compliance with internal standards as well. In the absence of a compliance officer, companies run the risk of violating applicable laws and regulations, thus exposing themselves to potential reputational damages and fines. Personal Competencies

    Career Pathways


  • Legal Associate / Junior Legal AdvisorStrong legal research and analytical skills
  • Excellent drafting and negotiation abilities
  • Corporate Legal Advisor Communication and advisory skills
  • Understanding of corporate law and governance

Key Responsibilities


  • Draft and review contracts and legal documents
  • Ensure regulatory compliance with local and international laws
  • Advise on mergers, acquisitions, and partnerships
  • Handle disputes and litigation in coordination with external counsel
  • Protect intellectual property and manage trademarks or patents

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details

Master's Degree in Law (LLM) Specialization in corporate law or international business law preferred

Where will you work?

Places of Work: Government Agencies, Private Firms, Committees for Social Welfare, Corporates, Non- Governmental Organizations and International Commissions among others Entrepreneurship: You can work as a consultant or a freelancer. Work Environment: You will work in the legal departments of large corporations, mostly in an office set up. You may have to go to the court if required. You will work for a minimum of 8 hours a day and are likely to put in extra hours depending on the workload or the number of clients being handled Opportunities for work exist for differently abled in this field.
