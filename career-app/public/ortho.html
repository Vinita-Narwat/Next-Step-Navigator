ORTHOPAEDIST

An orthopaedist (or orthopedic surgeon) specializes in diagnosing, treating, and preventing disorders of the bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, and spine. They manage conditions like fractures, arthritis, sports injuries, and congenital deformities, using both surgical and non-surgical methods.An Orthopaedist, is a medical professional who focuses on treating disorders and injuries of the musculoskeletal system comprising of the bones, muscles, ioints, and soft tissues.

    Career Pathways


  • General Orthopaedist:Treat common bone and joint conditions.
  • Orthopedic Surgeon:Perform surgeries like joint replacements, fracture repairs, and spinal corrections.
  • Sports Medicine Specialist: Manage athletic injuries and rehabilitation.
  • Pediatric Orthopaedist: Treat musculoskeletal issues in children.

Key Responsibilities


  • Deep knowledge of human anatomy and biomechanics
  • Surgical precision and decision-making
  • Diagnostic and imaging interpretation skills
  • Empathy and patient communication
  • Physical stamina and hand-eye coordination

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
MBBS 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology. Admission via NEET-UG. Duration: 5.5 years.
MS (Orthopaedics) Postgraduate degree after MBBS. Admission via NEET-PG. Duration: 3 years.
MCh / Fellowship Super-specialization in advanced orthopedic procedures. Entry via NEET-SS or institutional exams.

Fees

The approx. course fee ranges between INR 35,000-9,42,000* for the full course

*These figures are estimated numbers and will vary from Institute to Institute.

Where will you work?

Places of Work: Clinics, Hospitals, Nursing homes, Medical Colleges, Research Institutes and others. Entrepreneurship: You can set up your own private practice Work Environment: It is a desk job. You will need to handle a team of nurses and other medical staff. Local travel is not a part of this iob role. Part-time work and contractual jobs are available. Hospitals and clinics usually work for 6 to 7 days. You are likely to work 9 to 10 hours 6 days a week. This may vary from clinic to clinic. Shift system may be available. Opportunities for work exist for differently abled in this field

Expected Growth Path

Intern → Resident Orthopaedist → Orthopaedist → Senior Orthopaedic → Head of Orthopaedist Department → Dean of the Hospital

Expected Income

The approx. salary of an Orthopaedist ranges between INR 40,000 to 150,000* per month.
