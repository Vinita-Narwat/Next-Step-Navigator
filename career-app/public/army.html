INDIAN ARMY OFFICER

An Indian Army Officer is a leader, strategist, and protector of the nation—entrusted with commanding troops, managing operations, and upholding national security with honor and discipline.The Indian Army is the land based branch Of the Indian Armed Forces and is also the largest arm Of the same. The main duties of an Indian Army officer is to ensure national security, integrity and unity as well as also being responsible for defending the country against both internal and external threats and conduct humanitarian rescue operations whenever and wherever necessary.

    Career Pathways


  • Combat Arms:Infantry, Armoured Corps, Artillery
  • Combat Support Arms:Engineers, Signals, Air Defence
  • Services: Army Medical Corps, Ordnance, Intelligence, Education Corps
  • Special Forces: Para SF, NSG (after selection and training)

Key Responsibilities


  • Leadership and decision-making under pressure
  • Physical and mental endurance
  • Strategic thinking and discipline
  • Communication and team management
  • Adaptability to diverse terrains and missions

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
NDA (National Defence Academy) 10+2 with Physics & Mathematics
CDS (Combined Defence Services) Graduation in any discipline
TES (Technical Entry Scheme) 10+2 with 70% in PCM + JEE Mains

Fees

The approx. course fee ranges between INR 6,000 - 10,46,000*

*These figures are estimated numbers and will vary from Institute to Institute.

Where will you work?

Places of Work: You may expect to serve as a part of the regiment or lead regiments depending upon the rank held or serve in one of the training academies. Work Environment: The work timings are typically 6 to 7 days a week for 8 to 9 hours per day. Shift system is applicable. You are expected to deal with challenging situations. Most of the work is outdoors. You will be posted in different parts of India with frequent transfers. Opportunities for work exist for differently abled in this field

Expected Growth Path


Lieutenant → Captain → Major → Lt. Colonel→ Colonel → Brigadier → Major General → Lt. General → General → Field Marshal

Expected Income

The approx. salary Of an Indian Army Officer (gazetted) ranges between INR 56,100 - 2,50,000* per month.
