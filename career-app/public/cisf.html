CISF OFFICER

Central Industrial Security Force (or CISF) is another wing of the central armed police force and is the largest industrial security force, The primary objective of a CISF officer is to provide industrial units such as atomic power plants as well as other important areas for the Indian economy with military grade security and round the clock protection.
