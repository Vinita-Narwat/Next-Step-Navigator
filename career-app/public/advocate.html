ADVOCATE

An Advocate is a licensed legal practitioner who represents individuals, organizations, or the government in legal proceedings. They argue cases in court, draft legal documents, and advise clients on legal rights and obligations. Advocates play a vital role in upholding justice, interpreting laws, and ensuring fair representation in civil, criminal, and constitutional mattersA Lawyer is especially trained and licensed by a state to practice law. Lawyers provide legal advice and counsel to people, research and gather information or evidence in cases, draw up legal documents related to wills, divorces, contract, real estate transactions and so on. They also mediate disputes and prosecute or defend accused people in courts.

    Career Pathways


  • Junior Advocate / Legal InternStrong analytical and research skills
  • Excellent communication and argumentation
  • Advocate / Legal Associate Knowledge of legal procedures and statutes
  • Ethical judgment and confidentiality

Key Responsibilities


  • Represent clients in court during trials, hearings, and appeals
  • Provide legal advice on disputes, contracts, and regulatory matters
  • Draft legal documents such as petitions, affidavits, and agreements
  • Conduct legal research and prepare case strategies
  • Negotiate settlements and mediate conflicts

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB) Mandatory to become an advocate in India
Master's Degree in Law (LLM) Optional for specialization in fields like criminal, corporate, or constitutional law
Bar Council Registration Required to practice law and appear in court

Fees

The approx. course fee ranges between INR 12,000 - INR 7,56,000*

*These figures are estimated numbers and will vary from Institute to Institute.

Where will you work?

Places of Work: Legal Firms, Law Schools and Colleges, Government Agencies, all organizations such as Media Houses, Political Consultancies, Pharmaceuticals, IT firms etc have lawyers on their payrolls to advise them in legal matters. Entrepreneurship: After gaining a few years of experience you can set up your own private legal firm. Work Environment: You will work in an office set up, but you will also often travel for meetings or court proceedings. Work from home option is usually not available. You may expect to work beyond court-hours, which is usually 5 or 6 days a week and 7 or 8 hours every day. This may vary from court to court. Opportunities for work exist for differently abled in this field.

Expected Growth Path


Law Intern → Advocate → Senior Advocate
