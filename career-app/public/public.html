PUBLIC POLICY ADVISOR

A Public Policy Advisor provides expert guidance on policy development, implementation, and evaluation. They work with lawmakers, government agencies, advocacy groups, and private organizations to shape policies that address social, economic, and environmental challengesA Public Policy Advisor is a professional who analyzes the practical and functional aspects of policies in a specific field in government or a private organization. Policy advisors have expert knowledge on the legal aspects of policymaking. They advice on existing policies and explore opportunities for improvement or alteration. They encourage positive changes within organizations.

    Career Pathways


  • Policy Intern / Research AssistantAnalytical thinking and data interpretation
  • Strong writing and communication skills
  • Policy Analyst / Legislative Assistant Understanding of legislative and regulatory frameworks
  • Stakeholder engagement and diplomacy

Key Responsibilities


  • Research and analyze legislation, regulations, and political trends
  • Develop policy recommendations based on data and stakeholder input
  • Prepare briefing materials and reports for decision-makers
  • Engage with stakeholders including government officials, NGOs, and the public
  • Monitor policy outcomes and suggest improvements

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
10+2 (Higher Secondary) Foundation for undergraduate studies in public affairs
Bachelor's Degree Preferred in Public Policy, Political Science, Economics, Law, or Sociology
Master's Degree Ideal for advanced roles; degrees in Public Administration, Public Policy, or International Relations

Fees

Where will you work?

Places of Work: Government Agencies, Private Firms, Committees for Social Welfare, Corporates, and Non-Governmental Organizations among others. Entrepreneurship: You can work as a consultant or a freelancer Work Environment: You will work in the legal department of firms/agencies/companies. You may have to go to court if required, but most of the time is spent inside the office. You are likely to work over 40 hours a week, although you may also be required to work overtime depending on tasks and deadlines Opportunities for work exist for differently abled in this field

Expected Growth Path


Junior Legal Officer → Legal Officer → Senior legal officer → GM/Associate Vice President (Legal) → Vice President/President (Legal and Corporate Affairs)
