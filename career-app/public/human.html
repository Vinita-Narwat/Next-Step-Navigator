HUMAN RIGHTS LAWYER

A Human Rights Lawyer specializes in protecting and promoting the rights and freedoms guaranteed to individuals under national and international law. They may represent vulnerable populations, challenge government policies, or work with global organizations to uphold justice and dignity Human Rights Lawyers fight for individuals or groups of people who have been denied basic human rights that are guranteed by the Constitution of India. They work to ensure that the basic rights of every human being, especially disadvantaged groups such as LGBTQ, women, differently abled persons, ethnic and religious minorities etc. are protected.

    Career Pathways


  • Legal Intern / Research AssistantStrong legal research and writing
  • Empathy and cultural sensitivity
  • Human Rights Advocate / Legal Associate Public speaking and advocacy
  • Negotiation and conflict resolution

Key Responsibilities


  • Represent clients in cases involving discrimination, abuse, or denial of rights
  • Advocate for policy changes and legal reforms
  • Conduct legal research on human rights laws and precedents
  • Collaborate with NGOs and international bodies on human rights campaigns
  • Provide legal aid to marginalized communities

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB) Mandatory to practice law and specialize in human rights
Master's Degree in Law (LLM) Specialization in Human Rights Law or International Law preferred
Certification in Human Rights Law Offered by institutions like Amnesty International, UN, or online platforms

Where will you work?

Places of Work: Human Rights Courts, Public Prosecution, Legal Consultancy Firm, Non-governmental Organizations, Human Rights Agencies, Social Welfare Departments, Corporates, Media Houses Entrepreneurship: You can work as a consultant or a freelancer. Work Environment: This is not a desk job and you will need to travel to different geographies, even remote (e.g. for collecting evidences). You may have to work with a team with extended working hours. Opportunities for work exist for differently abled in this field.
