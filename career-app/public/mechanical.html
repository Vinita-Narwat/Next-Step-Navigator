MECHANICAL ENGINEER

Mechanical engineering applies principles of physics, mathematics, and materials science to solve problems related to motion, energy, and force. Mechanical engineers work across industries like automotive, aerospace, energy, manufacturing, robotics, and even biomedical devicesMechanical Engineers design, develop, build and test all sorts of mechanical devices, tools, engines and machines in just about every type of industry.

    Career Pathways


  • Design Engineer:Create blueprints and 3D models for machines and components.
  • Manufacturing Engineer:Optimize production processes and machinery.
  • Automotive Engineer: Design and test vehicles and their systems.
  • Aerospace Engineer: Work on aircraft, satellites, and propulsion systems.

Key Responsibilities


  • Proficiency in CAD/CAM tools
  • Strong grasp of mechanics and thermodynamics
  • Analytical and problem-solving abilities
  • Knowledge of materials and manufacturing processes
  • Team collaboration and project management

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
Diploma in Mechanical Engineering 10th pass from a recognized board. Entry into technician roles or lateral B.Tech admission.
B.E./B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering 10+2 with PCM. Admission via JEE Main, state CETs, or private university exams.
M.Tech/MS in Mechanical Engineering B.E./B.Tech in Mechanical or related field. Entry via GATE or university-specific exams.
