QUALITY ASSURANCE MANAGER

A Quality Assurance Manager ensures that products, services, or processes meet established standards of quality. They design testing procedures, lead QA teams, and implement systems to prevent defects and improve performance across departments.it is responsible for the planning and execution of tests on all products and services developed by a company. In IT, a Quality Assurance engineer finds and fixes bugs (or errors) in a product or program before its launch, working with developers to fix problems when necessary.

    Career Pathways


  • Quality Inspector / QA AnalystAttention to detail and analytical thinking

Key Responsibilities


  • Develop and implement QA policies and procedures
  • Monitor production processes to ensure compliance with standards
  • Lead quality audits and inspections
  • Analyze data and identify areas for improvement
  • Train staff on quality protocols
