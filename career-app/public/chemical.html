Chemical Engineering

"Chemical engineering is the bridge between science and practical innovation, turning raw materials into life-changing products." Chemical engineers are involved with the process of designing and readjusting the processes associated with the manufacture of medicines, packaged and unpackaged food, oil products like petroleum, LPG, kerosene, and other biological commodities, etc. They are most commonly employed by large-scale manufacturing industries to amplify the production and quality by reducing the cost of manufacture. Chemical engineers have a vast array of branches that correlate to their line of work, helping them branch out to many divisions.

  • Mass and Energy Balances: Understanding how materials and energy flow through systems.
  • Thermodynamics: Studying energy transformations and equilibrium.
  • Fluid Mechanics: Analyzing how fluids behave in motion and at rest.
  • Heat and Mass Transfer: Designing systems for efficient energy and material exchange.
  • Process Design: Creating safe, sustainable, and cost-effective chemical processes.

Applications


  • Petroleum and Petrochemicals
  • Pharmaceuticals and Healthcare
  • Food Processing
  • Polymers and Plastics
  • Environmental and Safety Engineering

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
10+2 (Science Stream) Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as core subjects with minimum 60% marks (may vary).
Entrance Exams JEE Main, JEE Advanced, or state-level engineering entrance exams.
Undergraduate Degree B.Tech/B.E in Chemical Engineering (4-year program).
Postgraduate Options M.Tech, M.E, or Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering or related fields.
