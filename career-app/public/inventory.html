INVENTORY MANAGER

An Inventory Manager oversees the storage, flow, and tracking of goods within a company. They ensure that inventory levels are optimized, stock is accurately recorded, and supply chain operations run smoothly.An Inventory Manager is responsible for directing and managing the tasks related to inventory management. They coordinate and plan the production plan, manage inventory, and schedule and plan the final product.

    Career Pathways


  • Inventory Assistant / StorekeeperAnalytical thinking and attention to detail
  • Knowledge of inventory software (e.g., SAP, Oracle, Zoho)
  • Inventory Analyst Strong organizational and planning skills
  • Problem-solving and decision-making

Key Responsibilities


  • Monitor stock levels and forecast demand
  • Track inventory movement across warehouses or stores
  • Coordinate with suppliers and procurement teams
  • Implement inventory control systems and software
  • Conduct audits and reconcile discrepancies

Eligibility Criteria

Bachelor's Degree Preferred in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Business Administration, or Commerce
Diploma in Inventory or Warehouse Management Offers practical training in stock control and logistics
