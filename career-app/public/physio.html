PHYSIOTHERAPIST

Physiotherapists are professionals who treat iniury and help restore movement and function affected by the iniury. Physiotherapists apply various techniques and methods such as massage and exercise and help patients manage pain, balance, mobility, and motor function.Physiotherapists help patients recover from injuries, surgeries, and chronic conditions by improving mobility, reducing pain, and restoring physical function. They use exercises, manual therapy, and equipment to treat musculoskeletal, neurological, and cardiopulmonary issues. Their work spans hospitals, clinics, sports centers, and rehabilitation facilities.

    Career Pathways


  • Clinical Physiotherapist:Treat patients in hospitals or clinics for orthopedic, neurological, or post-surgical recovery.
  • Sports Physiotherapist:Work with athletes to prevent and rehabilitate injuries.
  • Pediatric Physiotherapist: Help children with developmental or physical challenges.
  • Geriatric Physiotherapist: Support elderly patients with mobility and pain management.

Key Responsibilities


  • Strong understanding of anatomy and biomechanics
  • Manual therapy and exercise prescription
  • Empathy and patient motivation
  • Communication and observation skills
  • Familiarity with rehabilitation equipment and protocols

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
BPT 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology. Admission via state CETs or university exams. Duration: 4.5 years.
MPT BPT graduates. Admission via entrance exams or merit. Duration: 2 years.
Certifications Short-term courses in specialized therapy techniques. Useful for private practice and advanced roles.
