LOAN OFFICER

Loan officers offer loans and/or credit services to individuals and businesses. They are responsible for the evaluation and authorization of loans and various forms of credit.Loan officers evaluate, authorize, and recommend approval of loan applications for individuals and businesses. They work in banks, credit unions, NBFCs, and mortgage companies, helping clients secure personal, home, auto, or business loans.

    Career Pathways


  • Retail Banking:Personal loans, auto loans, education loans
  • Mortgage Lending: Home loans and refinancing
  • Commercial Lending: Business loans and credit lines
  • Microfinance & Rural Lending: Loans for agriculture and small enterprises

Key Responsibilities


  • Credit Analysis & Risk Assessment
  • Customer Relationship Management
  • Knowledge of Lending Regulations
  • Communication & Negotiation
  • Financial Software Proficiency (e.g., LOS, CRM tools)

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
Bachelor’s Degree In Finance, Economics, or Business Administration. Foundation for credit analysis and lending.
Certifications CMLO, LOC, or NISM modules for mortgage, personal, or business loan specialization.
Experience 1–3 years in banking, customer service, or financial advisory roles.
