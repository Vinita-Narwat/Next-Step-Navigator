CHARTERED ACCOUNTANT

Chartered Accountants are qualified to monitor a companVs finances, provide financial feedback and suggestions. They deal with four areas — taxation, financial accounting and reporting, applied finance, and management accounting.Chartered Accountants (CAs) are financial experts who manage accounting, auditing, taxation, and financial planning for individuals, businesses, and government bodies. It’s one of the most respected and rewarding professions in finance.

    Career Pathways


  • Audit & Assurance:Work with audit firms or corporates to verify financial records and ensure compliance.
  • Taxation: Specialize in direct and indirect taxes, GST, and international tax advisory.
  • Corporate Finance: Handle mergers, acquisitions, budgeting, and financial strategy for companies.
  • Management Consultancy: Advise businesses on improving efficiency, profitability, and financial planning.
  • Banking & Financial Services: Join banks, NBFCs, or investment firms in roles like credit analyst, risk manager, or financial advisor.

Key Responsibilities


  • Financial analysis and reporting
  • Tax planning and legal compliance
  • Auditing and risk management
  • Business strategy and ethics
  • Communication and client handling

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
CA Foundation Passed Class 12 (any stream, preferably Commerce). Register with ICAI and prepare for 4 papers.
CA Intermediate Passed Foundation or hold a Commerce degree with 55% (or any degree with 60%) for direct entry.
CA Final Passed Intermediate and completed 2.5 years of Articleship training.

Where will you work?

Places of Work: Chartered Accounting Firms, Business Consultancies, Companies across private and public sector.

Opportunities for work exist for differently abled in this field
