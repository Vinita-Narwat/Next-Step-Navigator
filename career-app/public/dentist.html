DENTIST

Dentist is a specialist trained to look after the oral health of human beings. They help patients keep their teeth, gums, and mouth healthy.Dentists diagnose and treat issues related to teeth, gums, and oral health. They perform procedures like fillings, root canals, extractions, and cosmetic treatments, while also educating patients on preventive care. Dentistry blends science, precision, and patient care.

    Career Pathways


  • General Dentist:Provide routine dental care and basic procedures.
  • Orthodontist:Specialize in braces and teeth alignment.
  • Periodontist: Treat gum diseases and perform surgeries.
  • Prosthodontist: Design and fit dental prosthetics like crowns and dentures.

Key Responsibilities


  • Manual dexterity and precision
  • Strong knowledge of anatomy and oral pathology
  • Communication and patient care
  • Problem-solving and diagnostic skills
  • Familiarity with dental tools and imaging technology

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
BDS 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology. Admission via NEET-UG. Duration: 5 years.
MDS Postgraduate degree after BDS. Admission via NEET-MDS. Duration: 3 years.
Certifications Short-term courses in cosmetic dentistry, implants, or laser procedures. Useful for private practice.

Fees

The approx. course fee ranges between INR 14,000-4,50,000* for the full course

*These figures are estimated numbers and will vary from Institute to Institute.

Where will you work?

Places of Work: Government and Private hospitals, Nursing homes, Colleges and Universities among others. Entrepreneurship: You can set up private practice in your own clinic. Work Environment: It is a desk job. You may be required to manage a team. Local travel is not part of the job. While hospitals and clinics are often open 24X7, you may work for 6 to 7 days a week and 9 to 10 hours every day. This may, however, vary from hospital to hospital. Opportunities for work exist for differently abled in this field

Expected Growth Path

Intern → Junior Doctor → Dentist Hospital/Clinic Owner
OR
Intern → Junior Doctor Dentist → Dental Surgeon → Head of the Department → Chief Medical Officer → Dean of the Hospital
