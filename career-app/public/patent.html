PATENT LAWYER

A Patent Lawyer specializes in intellectual property law, helping inventors and companies secure legal protection for their inventions. They draft and file patent applications, defend patents in court, and advise on patent strategy and infringement issues.A Patent Lawyer is mainly responsible for protecting the legal status of industrial or intellectual property rights. They advise their clients on design, invention, trademarks, know-hows as well as data, software and hardware protection mechanisms.

    Career Pathways


  • Legal Intern / IP AssociateStrong legal writing and analytical skills
  • Attention to detail and research ability
  • Patent Agent / Patent Analyst Understanding of national and international IP laws
  • echnical knowledge in science or engineeringT

Key Responsibilities


  • Draft and file patent applications with national and international patent offices
  • Conduct patent searches and assess novelty and eligibility
  • Advise clients on patentability and IP strategy
  • Handle patent litigation and infringement cases
  • Negotiate licensing agreements and IP transfers
