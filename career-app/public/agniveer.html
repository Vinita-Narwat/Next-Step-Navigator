AGNIVEER

Agniveer is a short-term service opportunity in the Indian Armed Forces under the Agnipath Scheme, launched by the Government of India. Selected candidates serve for 4 years in the Army, Navy, or Air Force, gaining military training, discipline, and experience. After completion, a portion may be retained for permanent service, while others transition to civilian careers with financial and skill-based support.Under the Agnipath scheme, people between the ages of 17 years and 23 years will be recruited into the military service of their choice for four years. After this tenure, 25% of the Agniveers will be retained and the remaining 75% will be permitted to return to civilian life. During this duration of 4 years, they would gain many skills which will help them to gain employment.

    Career Pathways


  • During Service (4 Years):Combat roles, technical support, logistics, aviation, naval operations, and ground duties
  • Military training, discipline, and exposure to defense systems
  • Post-Service Opportunities: Up to 25% may be selected for permanent roles in the armed forces
  • Others receive a Seva Nidhi package (~₹11.7 lakh) and skill certificates

Key Responsibilities


  • Physical fitness and endurance
  • Discipline and time management
  • Teamwork and leadership
  • Technical and combat training
  • Adaptability and resilience

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
Indian Army (General Duty) 10th pass with 45% aggregate and 33% in each subject
Indian Army (Technical) 10+2 with PCM + English (50% aggregate)
Indian Navy (SSR/MR) 10+2 with PCM (SSR); 10th pass (MR)

Fees

The approx. course fee ranges between INR 6,000 - 10,46,000*

*These figures are estimated numbers and will vary from Institute to Institute

Where will you work?

Places of Work: You will be placed under the Indian Navy, Indian Army, Indian Air Force as a non-commissioned officer Work Environment: The work timings are typically 6 to 7 days a week for 8 to 9 hours per day. Shift system is applicable. You are expected to deal with challenging situations. Most of the work is outdoors. Opportunities for work exist for differently abled in this field

Expected Growth Path


Private → Corporal → Sergeant → Sergeant Major
OR
Seaman → Leading Seaman → Petty Officer → Chief Petty Officer
OR
Aircraftman → Leading Aircraftman → Sergeant → Warrant Officer

Expected Income

The approx. salary of an Agniveer jawan ranges between INR 30,000 - 40,000* per month.
