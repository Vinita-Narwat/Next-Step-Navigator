SALES AND MARKETING MANAGER

A Sales and Marketing Manager is responsible for driving revenue growth by developing marketing strategies and managing sales operations. They align promotional efforts with business goals, analyze market trends, and lead teams to achieve targets.Sales and Marketing is an important corporate function focused on marketing the service/ product and converting demands into sales. Sales and marketing managers develop marketing strategy, conduct market research, develop promotions and advertising, handle public relations etc.

    Career Pathways


  • Sales Executive / Marketing AssociateStrategic thinking and planning
  • Sales forecasting and budgeting
  • Sales Manager / Brand Manager Digital marketing and analytics
  • Team leadership and motivation

Key Responsibilities


  • Develop marketing campaigns to promote products or services
  • Set and monitor sales targets for teams and regions
  • Conduct market research to identify customer needs and trends
  • Manage branding and advertising efforts
  • Coordinate with product, finance, and operations teams

Bachelor's Degree Preferred in Marketing, Business Administration, Commerce, or Economics
