FINANCIAL ANALYST

A financial Analyst is responsible for helping a company manage its overall financial status. They make use of the company's data to identify its profitability, liabilities, strengths, weaknesses, and future potential.inancial analysts evaluate economic trends, company performance, and investment opportunities to guide business decisions. They work in banks, investment firms, corporations, and government agencies, helping clients and executives make informed financial choices.

    Career Pathways


  • Equity Research Analyst:Analyze stocks and sectors for investment recommendations.
  • Credit Analyst:Assess creditworthiness of individuals or businesses.
  • Corporate Financial Analyst: Support budgeting, forecasting, and strategic planning within companies.
  • Investment Banking Analyst: Assist in mergers, acquisitions, and capital raising.

Key Responsibilities


  • Financial Modeling & Forecasting
  • Data Analysis & Interpretation
  • Knowledge of Financial Statements
  • Excel, Power BI, and Financial Software
  • Communication & Presentation Skills

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
Bachelor’s Degree In Finance, Economics, or related field. Foundation for financial modeling and analysis.
MBA or Master’s Degree Specialization in Finance or Investment Analysis. Preferred for senior roles.
Certifications (CFA, FRM) Optional but highly valued for credibility and global opportunities.
