BPO EXECUTIVE

BPO stands for Business Process Outsourcing. It is a method of subcontracting various business-related operations to third-party vendors- A BPO executive ensures BPO services meet the clients' needs. They communicate with vendors and attend to their queriesA BPO Executive handles customer interactions for outsourced business services, requiring strong communication skills and problem-solving abilities.     Career Pathways


  • Entry-Level:BPO Executive / Customer Support Associate
  • Role: Handle inbound/outbound calls, emails, or chats
  • Mid-Level: Senior Executive / Subject Matter Expert (SME)
  • Role: Handle complex queries, mentor new hires, support quality assurance
  • Team Leadership:Team Leader / Process Trainer
  • Role: Manage a team, monitor KPIs, conduct training sessions

Key Responsibilities


  • Manage customer interactions through calls, emails, and chats
  • Resolve complaints and queries promptly and professionally
  • Maintain records of customer interactions and transactions
  • Follow company protocols and communication guidelines
  • Meet performance targets such as response time and customer satisfaction

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
10+2 (Higher Secondary) Minimum requirement for entry-level voice-based BPO roles
Bachelor's Degree Preferred for non-voice, backend, or technical support roles (fields like Commerce, Business Administration, Marketing)
Diploma in Communication or Customer Service Enhances employability and communication skills for client-facing roles
