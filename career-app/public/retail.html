RETAIL MANAGER

A Retail Manager oversees the daily operations of a retail store, ensuring smooth functioning, high customer satisfaction, and achievement of sales targets. They manage staff, inventory, and customer service while implementing strategies to boost business performance.Retail Managers manage a retail outlet's day to day functioning around selling of goods and services. They are responsible for the success of any retail store. A retail manager supervises store employees, maintains customer satisfaction, and oversees inventory and stock management.

    Career Pathways


  • Sales Associate / Store ExecutiveLeadership and team management
  • Sales and customer service skills
  • Department Supervisor Communication and interpersonal skills
  • Inventory and merchandising knowledge

Key Responsibilities


  • Manage store operations including staffing, scheduling, and customer service
  • Monitor sales performance and set targets
  • Handle inventory and stock levels
  • Train and supervise staff
  • Ensure visual merchandising and store presentation

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
10+2 (Higher Secondary) Entry-level retail roles such as sales associate or cashier
Bachelor's Degree Preferred in Retail Management, Business Administration, or Commerce
Diploma in Retail Management Offers practical training in store operations and merchandising

Fees

Where will you work?

Places of Work: Retail companies-stores, Showrooms, Supermarkets, Department store, Small retail businesses. Work Environment: You will work in a desk and office oriented environment with supervisory responsibilities. You will need to supervise a team of Senior Executives and Executives. Some travelling may be involved. You are expected to work 5 to 6 days a week and 8 to 9 hours per day. This varies from organization to organization. Opportunities for work exist for differently abled in this field.

Expected Growth Path


Salesperson → Sales Supervisor, Retail Trade → Manager, Retail Trade → General Manager, Retail Trade → Director, Retail Trade → Working Proprietor, Retail Trade
