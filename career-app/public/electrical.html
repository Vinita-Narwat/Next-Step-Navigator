ELECTRICAL ENGINEER

The concepts of electrical and electronics both deal with electricity. Electrical devices produce voltage and current and electronic devices control the voltage and current. Hence, electrical engineers are primarily concerned with the generation, transmission and distribution of electrical power. Whereas electronic engineers design, develop and test components, devices, systems or equipment that use electricity as part of their source of power.lectrical engineers work on technologies that power homes, industries, and digital systems. Their expertise spans power generation, electronics, control systems, telecommunications, and embedded systems. They play a vital role in modernizing infrastructure and driving innovation.

    Career Pathways


  • Power Systems Engineer:Design and manage power generation and distribution networks.
  • Control Systems Engineer:Develop automation and robotics systems.
  • Electronics Engineer: Work on embedded systems, consumer electronics, and circuit design.
  • Telecommunications Engineer: Handle network infrastructure and signal processing.

Key Responsibilities


  • Circuit design and simulation
  • Power system analysis
  • Programming (C/C++, Python, MATLAB)
  • Knowledge of electrical codes and safety standards
  • Problem-solving and analytical thinking
