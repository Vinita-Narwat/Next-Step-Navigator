SOFTWARE ENGINEER

A software engineer designs, develops, tests, and maintains software systems and applications that power everything from mobile apps to enterprise platforms. It’s one of the most in-demand and versatile careers in tech.A Computer Programmer designs, develops and tests software that meet their client's needs. They also search for bugs in the program and try to remove those bugs to improve the overall Utility of the program.

    Career Pathways


  • Frontend Developer:Builds user interfaces using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, etc.
  • Backend Developer:Manages server-side logic, databases, and APIs.
  • Full Stack Developer: Combines frontend and backend skills.
  • Mobile App Developer: Creates apps for Android/iOS using Kotlin, Swift, or Flutter.

Key Responsibilities


  • Programming Languages: Python, Java, C++, JavaScript, etc.
  • System Design & Architecture
  • Team Collaboration & Agile Methodologies
  • Version Control (Git), Testing & Debugging
  • Problem-Solving & Algorithms

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
Bachelor’s Degree In Computer Science, IT, or related field. Covers programming, databases, and software design.
Postgraduate Degree (Optional) M.Tech/MS in Software Engineering or related field. Preferred for research or leadership roles.
Certifications (Optional) Cloud, full stack, or language-specific certifications for specialization and career growth.
