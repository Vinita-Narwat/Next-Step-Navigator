AERONAUTICAL ENGINEER

Aeronautical engineers specialize in the design, development, testing, and maintenance of aircraft and their systems. They focus on aerodynamics, propulsion, avionics, and structural integrity to ensure safe and efficient flight. Their work spans civil aviation, defense, and research sectors.An aeronautical engineer is responsible for designing, developing and constructing safe and fuel-efficient machines, which can fly in air and space such as aircraft, helicopters, satellites, missiles, drones and spacecraft. He/she is also responsible for regularly inspecting and issuing clearance of the aircraft for air-worthiness.

    Career Pathways


  • Aircraft Design Engineer:Develop airframes, wings, and control surfaces.
  • Propulsion Engineer:Work on jet engines, fuel systems, and thrust mechanisms.
  • Flight Test Engineer: Analyze aircraft performance during test flights.
  • Avionics Engineer: Design electronic systems for navigation, communication, and flight control.

Key Responsibilities


  • Strong grasp of aerodynamics and fluid mechanics
  • Proficiency in simulation and modeling software
  • Knowledge of aircraft systems and safety standards
  • Analytical thinking and problem-solving
  • Team collaboration and project management

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Details
Diploma in Aeronautical Engineering 10th pass from a recognized board. Entry into technician roles or lateral B.Tech admission.
B.E./B.Tech in Aeronautical Engineering 10+2 with PCM. Admission via JEE Main, state CETs, or private university exams.
M.Tech/MS in Aeronautical Engineering B.E./B.Tech in Aeronautical or Mechanical Engineering. Entry via GATE or university-specific exams.

Where will you work?

Places of Work: Indian Air Force, Defence Ministry, Airlines industry, Aviation companies or Research agencies. Work Environment: It is a full-time job, work hours may extend to more than 8 hours a day. You can either work in offices or at units where equipment is designed, manufactured, assembled or tested. Opportunities for differently abled exist in this field
